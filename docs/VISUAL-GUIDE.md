## Color Scheme

### Light Theme (Default)
- **Card Background**: White (#ffffff)
- **Card Border**: Light gray (#edebe9)
- **Card Border (Hover)**: Primary blue (#0078d4)
- **Icon Circle Background**: Light blue (#eff6fc)
- **Icon Color**: Primary blue (#0078d4)
- **Title Text**: Dark gray (#323130)
- **Description Text**: Medium gray (#605e5c)
- **Shadow**: rgba(0,0,0,0.1) → rgba(0,0,0,0.15)

### Dark Theme (Automatic)
- **Card Background**: Dark gray
- **Card Border**: Darker gray
- **Icon Circle Background**: Darker blue
- **Icon Color**: Light blue
- **Title Text**: White
- **Description Text**: Light gray
- **Shadow**: Adjusted for dark background

## Animation Timing

```
Initial Load:
  Fade in: 300ms ease-out
  
Card Hover:
  Transform: 200ms ease-in-out
  Box-shadow: 200ms ease-in-out
  Border-color: 200ms ease-in-out
  
Click:
  Scale down: 100ms
  Scale up: 100ms
  
Focus:
  Outline: immediate
```
